    GUIDEBOOK

    Play using your keyboard or the on-screen keys.

    Take note:

    • 1. Use digits for numbers (E.g. 9 not Nine)
    • 2. Use "×" on the screen or "*" on your keyboard for multiplication
    • 3. Use "÷" on the screen or "/" on your keyboard for division
    • 4. Use "^" for power (E.g. 9 to the power of 2 is 9^2)
    • 5. Use "Half" for 1/2 (E.g. Half not 1/2 or half or 0.5)
